# Music channels need no rights-clearance step beyond the existing embed-only model

v1's risk list named "Music channels wait for clearance" as a standing blocker, written before the embed-only (never-rehosted) mechanics were fully reasoned through. Every channel — Weddings, Kebire, and any future Music channel — only ever embeds a video that's already public and embeddable on YouTube; if it's embeddable there, the uploader already carries whatever rights exposure comes with it, and that exposure doesn't change by genre. A Music channel is therefore governed by the exact same safeguards as every other channel (public + embeddable + the reactive block list), with no music-specific clearance step added on top. This reverses v1's risk-list entry; a takedown or rights dispute on music content is handled by the existing same-day blocklist process, same as any other channel.
